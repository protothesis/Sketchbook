Galleries

    Ctrl/⌘-click or Shift-click thumbnails to select, then drag onto a gallery, or onto + New gallery to make one. B adds to the target gallery (◉).

    Point me at your images.

    Add a folder (e.g. your ComfyUI output folder or a photo archive). It's scanned recursively. Add as many folders as you like and browse them all together or one at a time.

    Strictly read-only — nothing is ever moved, copied, renamed or deleted. Ratings, notes and galleries live in this browser's storage and can be exported as JSON.

    Settings

    Folders

    The disk path is only used to build copyable paths (browsers can't see it). Removing a folder forgets its file list and thumbnails; its ratings, notes and galleries come back if you add it again.
