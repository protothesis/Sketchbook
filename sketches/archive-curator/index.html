ArchiveCurator

Point me at an image archive.

Pick a folder (e.g. your ComfyUI output folder). It's scanned recursively and served back to you ten random images at a time. Rate them, note them, group them into virtual galleries.

Strictly read-only — nothing is ever moved, copied, renamed or deleted. Ratings, notes and galleries live in this browser's storage and can be exported as JSON.
