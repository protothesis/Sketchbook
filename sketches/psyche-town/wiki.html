Everything the simulation is made of. Each person's psyche is a little solar system: the ego is the sun (shaped by an Enneagram type), wrapped in the persona's shield, with a handful of archetypes orbiting as planets. Their pull on the sun decides who's in charge, and the angles between them make the weather. This is a sketch, not a clinical model; it's a way to feel how much of what we do is driven by forces we didn't choose.
