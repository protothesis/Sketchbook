Everyone here believes they're steering. Inside each person is a little solar system: the ego as the sun and a handful of archetypes as planets, each with its own orbit and its own swelling and shrinking. When a planet's pull beats the ego's hold, it takes the wheel, and the person's colour shows who's driving. Almost every grip passes on its own; what matters is whether anything irreversible happens first.

Tap anyone to see their orrery, or open the Lab to study one psyche up close.
