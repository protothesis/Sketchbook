Grow

Opens doors in bulk and keeps every room: no rerolls, faints or waypoints. Caps above still apply. The route seeds which doors open and what lies behind them: the same root seed, size, order and route always grow the same dungeon. On a map that's only a grow, changing the order, size or route grows the seed again, so you can flip between orders to compare. Grow from selected adds rooms anywhere in the room's branch (it and every room opened beyond it), keeping your view; new rooms are outlined.

      All regions

        Drawn under the rooms: each region's ground reaches a cell past its rooms, outlined just inside its edge. Hatched: ground a cloister has claimed and not built yet. Point at a region on the map to see its label; click it (a room, or its margin) to pin it, and click empty ground to clear the pins. Labels say more the closer you zoom. 7/12 means 7 rooms spent of a 12-room budget: once spent, every door leads out of the region. Hover any line here, or the region, structure and mood in the inspector, to highlight on the map: structure types always in type colours, moods in mood colours.

        Room contents

        Where things start, as each room's seed places them (engine/contents.js). Hover a name to preview it on the map, or to pick it out if it's on. Creatures: ghost · pixie · creeper. Features: sigil ◆, well ○, chests, vessels, sconces, hall waystones. Crates move; static blocks (darker = taller) don't. Zoomed far out, only creatures, locks, push blocks, sigils and wells show.
